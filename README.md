# Privacy Policy - MindBreeze Tile Match

**Last updated:** October 2026

**MindBreeze: Tile Match** is an offline puzzle game built to provide entertainment without collecting sensitive user information.

## 1. Information Collection & Use
MindBreeze does not require user registration or personal account creation. We do not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, location, or contact details.

## 2. Local Data Storage
Game statistics, best scores, selected themes, and difficulty preferences are stored locally on your device. This data is not transmitted to external servers and can be reset anytime within the game's settings menu.

## 3. Third-Party Services
The app operates fully offline and does not integrate third-party analytics trackers, background location tools, or data harvesting SDKs.

## 4. Children's Privacy
MindBreeze is suitable for users of all ages. Because we do not collect any personal data, our app fully complies with general children's privacy protection guidelines.

## 5. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
